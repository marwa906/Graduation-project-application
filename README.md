# Graduation-project-application
CG.OI is a smart application designed specifically to support graduates and students throughout their career journey, starting from identifying the most suitable career path to finding the right job and internship opportunities.  The app aims to help users build a strong professional future by providing tools and services that support skill development, resume improvement, interview preparation, and discovering job and training opportunities based on their major and interests.  CG.OI offers a smooth and fast user experience, acting as a complete career guide that helps graduates make the right decisions and continuously improve themselves.
